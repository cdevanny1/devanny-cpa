What should I bring to my first tax appointment?

Start with last year’s return and any tax forms you’ve received so far. Once we understand your situation, we can share a more tailored checklist.

Can you help if I’m self-employed?

Yes, self-employed individuals and small business owners are welcome to reach out. We can discuss your business, records, and what kind of support makes sense.

Do you offer help outside of tax season?

Tax questions don’t only come up at filing time. Ask about year-round tax planning or bookkeeping support for your situation.

Can I send my tax documents by email?

Please don’t send sensitive tax or financial information by regular email. Ask about the firm’s secure document-sharing options before sending anything.
